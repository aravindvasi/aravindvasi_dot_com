About

I'm an Electronics & Communication engineer based in Bengaluru, drawn to the places where machine learning, quantum computing, and signal intelligence overlap. I like systems that have to behave well under uncertainty — biological signals, noisy sensors, qubits.

By day I work as an SAP Digital Manufacturing support analyst at TCS, stabilising integrations and chasing down root causes. Outside that, I'm spending most of my time on quantum algorithms, ML research, and preparing for graduate school.

When I'm not in front of a terminal, I'm watching Formula One, tinkering with gadgets, or making art.
